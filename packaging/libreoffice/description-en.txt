Kannada spell checking for LibreOffice. Kannada joins case endings, auxiliaries and the copula to the word before them, so this dictionary describes Kannada inflection as rules and accepts the inflected forms: ಮರಗಳಲ್ಲಿ, ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿದೆ, ಮಾಡಲಾಗಿದೆ. It accepts 88.9% of the words in held-out Kannada Wikipedia text.

It holds 2 word lists. kn_IN.dic contains information from Alar by V. Krishna (https://alar.ink), made available under the Open Database License 1.0 (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). kn_IN-wikipedia.dic includes words counted in Kannada Wikipedia (https://kn.wikipedia.org), written by its contributors, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
