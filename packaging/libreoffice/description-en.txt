Kannada spell checking for LibreOffice. Kannada joins case endings, auxiliaries and the copula to the word before them, so this dictionary describes Kannada inflection as rules and accepts the inflected forms: ಮರಗಳಲ್ಲಿ, ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿದೆ, ಮಾಡಲಾಗಿದೆ. It accepts 70.8% of the words in held-out Kannada Wikipedia text.
